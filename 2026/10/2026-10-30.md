## 2. moveit 실습

- 실습에 installation을 따라하고 있었는데 urdf의 stl 경로 문제로 moveit setting에 stl이 보이지 않거나 moveit을 완성하고 rviz로 moveit plan excute 했을때 동작하지 않음
- 아래와 같은 파일들을 ai가 생성해
```
1 → No controller config (ros2_controllers.yaml), so joint_states never come in. The log shows The 'type' param was not defined for 'joint_state_broadcaster', and then the spawner died. That means /joint_states isn’t being published at all, so move_group can’t tell what pose the arm is in, and you get plan() failed or timeout reached.

2 → No moveit_controllers.yaml, so even a successful plan has nothing to execute it. This happens when the Setup Assistant’s “ROS 2 Controllers” and “MoveIt Controllers” tabs are skipped.

3 → kinematics.yaml is empty ({}), so there’s no IK. The log shows No kinematics plugins defined and No active joints or end effectors found for group 'arm'. That’s why the interactive marker (ball) doesn’t appear when you drag the arm, and pose goals don’t work.

→ One more thing needed: passive joints for the 4 steer and 4 wheel joints. Only the arm and gripper are in ros2_control, so those 8 joints never publish state, and MoveIt keeps seeing the current state as “incomplete”. Register them as passive joints in the SRDF.

→ Watch out with 5-DOF IK. The arm has 5 axes, so KDL usually fails to solve a full position + orientation goal. You’ll need position_only_ik: true in kinematics.yaml, or joint-space goals.

Once the fix is in, stop the running demo.launch.py (Ctrl+C), then rebuild and launch it again.
```
- cuMotion 역시 동작하지 않는중.
